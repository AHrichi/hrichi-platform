Get started

  1. Pair your devices

    Open Settings → Devices and press “Send me a code”. The code arrives in your Telegram chat; type it here. Pair your phone and your computer the same way and they stay in sync.

  2. Capture an idea

    In the Inbox, type what you want to remember and press Save (or Ctrl/⌘ + Enter). Tap the mic to talk, or hold it and let go to save a voice note of up to two minutes. You can also share text from your phone's share sheet once the app is installed, send it to your Telegram bot, or send it from StudyLM.

  3. Keep the drafts you want

    The AI turns a capture into one to three concepts, each with a plain explanation and recall questions. They wait under “New ideas to keep”: swipe right or press Keep, swipe left or press Discard, or ask for something simpler. Drafts you leave alone are kept after 48 hours.

The daily set

Today holds a short set: 5, 7 or 10 cards (choose in Settings), about three minutes. Answer in your head or type it, then reveal the answer and grade yourself:

  • → or swipe right: got it
  • ↑ or swipe up: shaky
  • ← or swipe left: missed
  • Space reveals the answer

A missed card comes back once at the end with another question. The schedule (FSRS) spaces each concept out as you remember it better. New concepts start the day after you keep them, at most two a day, and some cards ask you to teach it back out loud or to compare two related ideas. When nothing is due, opening the app still counts.

Streaks and the garden

  • A day counts when you answer at least one card. Every seven days in a row earns a freeze (two at most), used by itself on a day you miss.
  • No freeze left? Finish two sets the next day to repair the streak, once a month.
  • The garden grows a plant per topic and a leaf per concept: seed, sprout, leaf, bloom, golden, as your memory of it lasts longer. A drooping leaf is fading; it will be in a set soon.
  • In Settings: set size, new concepts a day, Telegram reminders, a Sunday recap, and an away mode that pauses everything.

With StudyLM

StudyLM teaches; the Learning Engine makes it stick. They are separate apps that share your reviews in one of two ways.

Linked (recommended)

  1. In StudyLM, open Progress and press “Connect to my daily reviews”.
  2. This app opens on its “Link StudyLM” page. On a paired device, press “Link StudyLM”; you go back to StudyLM. Do this once per browser.

From then on, with nothing to tap:

  • when you mark a lesson complete, its cards come here (lessons you finished before linking come too);
  • a flashcard you rate “Again” and a quiz question you get wrong come here as cards, so what you miss comes back;
  • they arrive as drafts in the Inbox and are kept by themselves after 48 hours;
  • once a card is kept, your flashcard ratings and quiz answers on it in StudyLM count as reviews: they move its schedule and your streak. Answers on questions the engine doesn't track don't count, because it has to know which idea you recalled;
  • StudyLM's Progress screen shows your streak, today's set and how its cards grow.

To stop, press “Disconnect” in StudyLM, or unlink it under Settings → Devices.

Not linked

“Learn this” on a flashcard or “Add to my daily reviews” on a lesson opens this app's Inbox, which asks before saving anything.

Your data

Everything lives on your device first and works offline. Paired devices sync through your own server; reminders come from a small worker that only knows your schedule. Voice notes are deleted once transcribed.
