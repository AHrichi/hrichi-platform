Exercise: The Power of Signals

Exploring the core primitives of Angular Signals and their interoperability with RxJS.

1. Writable Signals: signal()

These are the sources of truth in our application. We can directly change their values.

{{ quantity() }}

2. Computed Signals: computed()

These derive their value from other signals. They are read-only and update automatically and efficiently.

{{ fullName() }}
{{ isStockLow() }}
{{ stockMessage() }}

3. Effects: effect()

This code runs whenever any of its dependent signals change. Ideal for side effects like logging, analytics, or data synchronization.

4. RxJS Interop: toSignal()

Convert an RxJS Observable into a read-only Signal to seamlessly integrate with a modern, signal-based component.

Analogy: Subscribing to a magazine (the Observable). Each time a new issue is released, a delivery person (toSignal) puts it in your mailbox (the Signal), where you can check for the latest issue at any time.
