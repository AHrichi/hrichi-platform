iOS / iPhone

How to Export from WhatsApp on iOS

  1. Open the conversation in WhatsApp and tap the contact or group name at the top.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Export Chat.
  3. Select Without Media (recommended) or Attach Media—ChatPulse automatically skips heavy media inside the .zip.
  4. Save the .zip file and drop it into the box above.
Android

How to Export from WhatsApp on Android

  1. Open the chat, tap the ⋮ three-dot menu in the top-right corner, and choose More.
  2. Tap Export chat and select Without media.
  3. Save or transfer the exported .txt or .zip file to your device.
  4. Drop the file above—Android 12h/24h dash headers are detected automatically.
