How to Use the App

Learn how to create financial scenarios, track transactions, read analytics, and compare plans.

1. Getting Started

Create a Scenario

From the home page, type a name for your financial scenario (e.g., "My Retirement Plan") and click Get Started. You can create multiple scenarios to compare different strategies.

  • Duplicate a scenario to use it as a template
  • Rename or delete scenarios from the scenario selector
  • Switch between scenarios anytime — each keeps its own data and projections

2. Scenario Builder

The builder has four tabs. Fill them in order:

⚙️ Settings

Set your starting balance, monthly income, monthly expenses, years to project, and expected annual return rate. These form the foundation of your projection.

  • Starting Balance — your current savings
  • Monthly Income / Expenses — regular cash flow
  • Years — how far to project into the future
  • Annual Return — expected investment growth rate (e.g., 5% for conservative, 8% for moderate)

📈 Incomes

Add additional income sources beyond your monthly income (e.g., bonuses, rental income, side gigs). Each income can have a custom amount, start/end month, and annual escalation.

📉 Expenses

Add planned expenses — both one-time (e.g., buying a car) and recurring (e.g., insurance). Set the amount, timing, and annual escalation to model inflation.

🎯 Goals

Define financial goals like saving for a house, retirement, or education fund. Each goal has a target amount, target date, and monthly contribution. The app tracks progress toward each goal.

3. Daily Ledger

The ledger lets you log your actual income and expenses day by day. This creates a reality track that the app compares against your plan.

  • Add transactions with amount, type (income/expense), and optional notes
  • View your balance and recent activity
  • Compare actual spending against your monthly budget

4. How to Read the Analytics

The analytics page provides three tabs with detailed financial insights:

📊 Overview Tab

KPI Cards

At the top you'll see: Current Balance, Monthly Income, Monthly Expenses, Monthly Net (income minus expenses), Final Reserve (projected ending balance), and Goal Funding (total allocated to goals). Hover over any card to see the full value as a tooltip.

  • Green = positive / surplus
  • Red = negative / deficit
  • Amber = goal-related metrics

Plan vs Reality

Compares your projected balance and monthly cash flow against actual values from your ledger. ▲ Green means you're ahead of plan; ▼ Red means behind.

This Month

Shows income and expense progress for the current month: actual vs planned amounts, plus a net result bar. Use this to track your monthly budget in real time.

Charts

Four full-width charts give you a visual overview:

  • Capital Trajectory — line chart showing your projected balance over time
  • Income vs Expenses — bar chart comparing monthly inflows and outflows
  • Outflow Breakdown — doughnut chart showing expense categories as proportions
  • Goal Progress — bar chart of goal funding progress

📈 Trajectory Tab

  • Capital Growth Trajectory — a large full-width chart showing your accumulated capital over the entire projection period, with goal achievement markers
  • KPI cards show Total Inflow, Total Outflow, Reserve after Goals, and Goal Funding
  • Use the Monthly / Yearly / All toggle to view data at different granularities

🎯 Goals Tab

  • Score Rings — circular progress indicators showing your overall goal completion percentage for both the plan (purple) and reality (green)
  • Goal Breakdown — counts of achieved, on-track, at-risk, and behind-schedule goals
  • Goal Progress Overview — bar chart comparing planned amount, funded amount, and reality for each goal
  • Goal Milestones Table — detailed list of every goal with target amount, monthly contribution, target date, dual progress bars (planned vs reality), and status badges

5. Detailed Comparison

The Detailed Comparison page lets you compare two scenarios side by side. Select two scenarios and view:

  • Side-by-side KPI cards
  • Overlaid capital trajectory charts
  • Income, expense, and goal comparison tables
  • Combined goal progress visualization

Use this to evaluate different strategies (e.g., "aggressive investing" vs "conservative saving").

6. Data Management

💾 Export / Import

Use the header buttons to back up all your data as a JSON file or restore from a previous backup.

☁️ Google Drive Backup

Connect your Google Drive for automatic cloud backups. Configure a Client ID in Google Cloud Console, paste it into the Drive modal, and click Connect.

7. Tips & Shortcuts

  • 🧮 Calculator — click the calculator icon in the header to open a floating calculator widget
  • Theme toggle — switch between light and dark mode anytime
  • Copy scenarios — duplicate an existing scenario as a starting point for a new strategy
  • Hover tooltips — hover over any KPI value on the analytics page to see the full amount
  • Responsive design — the app works on desktop and tablet screens
